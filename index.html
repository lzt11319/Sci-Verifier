SciVeriRub: Co-Evolving Verifiers and Rubricators for Scientific Image Generation

Jiali Chen*, Zhengteng Lin*, Zuqi Wang*, Shirong Lin, Xi Yu, Yi Cai
South China University of Technology
Preprint coming soon
*Indicates Equal Contribution
Verifier Bench Rubric Bench

Overview of SciVeriRub: one-round co-evolving RL starts from task-specific SFT,
links Rubricator-generated rubric points with Verifier rollouts, and improves both models.

Abstract

Scientific image generation requires more than visual plausibility: generated diagrams must satisfy fine-grained domain constraints such as symbolic relations, structural consistency, factual knowledge, and multi-step reasoning requirements. Existing text-image metrics and preference-based evaluation struggle to capture such semantic correctness, while general-purpose VLM judges are costly and not optimized for scalable scientific image assessment.

We introduce SciVeriRub, a framework that co-evolves Verifiers and Rubricators for scientific image generation. The Rubricator decomposes each generation or editing instruction into structured evaluation points, while the Verifier predicts correctness and provides explanations and edit suggestions.

We construct a 74K-scale scientific image verification dataset and a balanced benchmark covering instruction following, knowledge, and reasoning. Based on Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct, we train Verifier and Rubricator models with supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning. For Verifier training, we propose an outcome-anchored Adaptive Reward Control scheme that prioritizes answer correctness and uses rubric coverage only as conditional shaping through dual reward channels. For Rubricator training, Verifier rollouts are used to identify effective rubric points whose coverage correlates with improved answer correctness.

Experiments show that our 8B Verifier approaches strong proprietary VLM judges on answer and explanation metrics, while our Rubricator achieves strong recall, non-hallucination rate, and structural F1. Furthermore, by using the Verifier’s judgments and edit suggestions as feedback in a verification-guided image editing loop, SciVeriRub can guide iterative refinement and improve the quality of generated scientific images.

Dataset and Results

Dataset composition first, followed by Verifier and Rubricator evaluation results.

Verifier data coverage. The training set contains 74,067 labeled verifier samples from 9 sources, spanning Instruction-Following, Knowledge, and Reasoning with representative examples from each source.

Verifier main results. With scores normalized to Gemini-3-Flash, RL improves our 8B Verifier over SFT-only on Answer, Explanation, and Edit, especially bringing Answer and Explanation close to strong proprietary judges.

Rubricator main results. RL also improves the Rubricator, yielding the strongest overall coverage, hallucination control, and structural quality with cleaner and more reliable rubric outputs.
